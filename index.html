About our founder

A name built on trust since 1998.

TEEKAY HARDWARE & PLYWOOD was founded in 1998 and was originally known as TEEKAY GLASS HOUSE. Step by step, it has grown into a trusted name for plywood, multiwood, laminated sheets, hardware, kitchen accessories and glass.

Through every stage of that journey, TK Abdul Salam has worked relentlessly to deliver high-quality products to customers at the best price – because lasting businesses are built on honest dealing, dependable quality and relationships that last long after the sale.
